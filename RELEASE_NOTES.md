Hafþi beta 16 for Apple Silicon Macs (macOS 13 or later).

- Download `Hafthi-0.1.0-beta.16-macOS-arm64.zip`, unzip it, and drag `Hafþi.app` to Applications. No Xcode or build tools are needed.
- Rename the optional tools section in Preferences to **Integrations**, including the back button.
- Keep the Yazi, Micro, Sampler, Fish, Starship and tgpt integrations from beta 15.

This beta is ad hoc signed but not notarized with an Apple Developer ID. macOS may block it on first launch. After trying to open it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway to approve this specific app.

Source code: https://github.com/pdahlbeck/hafthi-mac
