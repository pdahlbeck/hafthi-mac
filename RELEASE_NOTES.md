Hafþi beta 6 for Apple Silicon Macs (macOS 13 or later).

- Download `Hafthi-0.1.0-beta.6-macOS-arm64.zip`, unzip it, and drag `Hafþi.app` to Applications. No Xcode or build tools are needed.
- The Plugins overview now has separate Fish, Starship, and tgpt cards, each opening its own settings page with a back button.
- The right-click command-help action opens tgpt's page directly and focuses its question field. Existing settings continue to work.

This beta is ad hoc signed but not notarized with an Apple Developer ID. macOS may block it on first launch. After trying to open it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway to approve this specific app.

Source code: https://github.com/pdahlbeck/hafthi-mac
