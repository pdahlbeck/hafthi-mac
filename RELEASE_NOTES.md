Hafþi beta 5 for Apple Silicon Macs (macOS 13 or later).

- Download `Hafthi-0.1.0-beta.5-macOS-arm64.zip`, unzip it, and drag `Hafþi.app` to Applications. No Xcode or build tools are needed.
- Preferences now use a compact dark window with a sidebar like the Linux version: Appearance, Terminal, Background, and Plugins.
- Fish, Starship, and tgpt settings are grouped under Plugins. The tools remain optional and installed separately. Existing settings and terminal behavior continue to work as before.
- The right-click command-help action opens the Plugins page and focuses its question field.

This beta is ad hoc signed but not notarized with an Apple Developer ID. macOS may block it on first launch. After trying to open it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway to approve this specific app.

Source code: https://github.com/pdahlbeck/hafthi-mac
